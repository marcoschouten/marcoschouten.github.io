Marco Schouten

I am a PhD student in computer vision at DTU Copenhagen and the Pioneer Centre for AI (Belongie Lab). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof Dim Papadopoulos and Prof Serge Belongie. I am also a visiting researcher with Prof David Picard at École nationale des ponts et chaussées in Paris. Previously, I was a research scientist intern at Adobe Research Paris working in Prof Tamy Boubekeur's team. I recevied my master's degree in machine learning from KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm. In my free time, I enjoy drawing.
